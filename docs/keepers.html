Keeper War Room

Keeper candidates for every team in both leagues, graded by surplus value two ways: against your board (default sort) and against Sleeper ADP. K rd = the round he would fall to in this league's keeper draft (predicted keepers eat their cost-round slots first). Click a column to sort, click a team chip to scout a rival. A yellow K means the player was kept last year, so his cost escalates one round earlier. GGG cap math lives on the GGG league site — this page is about value.
