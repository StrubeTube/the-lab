Trade Lab

Build a trade and see who wins. Pre-draft, a player is worth his keeper surplus — the same draft-slot numbers as the Keepers page — because rosters reset at the draft. Picks are priced off this league's own trade history: what round of pick has actually bought a keeper at what cost. The keeper-impact panel shows whether an incoming keeper beats one either side already has, and the market lines show what a keeper like yours has really fetched.
