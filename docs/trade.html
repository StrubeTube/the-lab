Trade Lab

Build a trade and see who wins — in points over replacement, on the lens that matters right now. Pre-draft, a player is worth his keeper surplus (his projection minus the pick round he costs) because rosters reset at the draft; in-season he's worth his rest-of-season value with a nod to next year's keep. Picks are worth what's actually left on the board in that round of this league's keeper draft, and every offer is checked against the league's real trade history.
